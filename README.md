## 💼 What I'm working on

- **CoRe, student assistant.** Building a local-first AI agent that runs on our own GPU servers (FastAPI, vLLM), and supporting our intern cohort with their prototypes: OCR pipelines, data normalization, fine-tuning and documentation.
- **[Data anonymization](https://github.com/Riteshbhandarii/data-anonymization) (CoRe R&D).** Part of a team working on sanitizing company data (documents, spreadsheets, metadata and more) before it is sent to public AI models, and measuring whether the result can still be traced back.
- **Thesis: [DPPM](https://github.com/Riteshbhandarii/DPPM).** Dismantler Price Prediction Model. Predicting used car spare-part prices from scraped marketplace listings and vehicle registry data, with a full scraping → cleaning → modelling pipeline.
- **Federated learning privacy research.** Co-authoring a paper on gradient-inversion attacks, showing how much private training data can be reconstructed from shared gradients.
